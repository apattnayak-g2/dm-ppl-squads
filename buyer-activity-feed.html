What this is and open questions

  1. 1
    A separate feed, not a new queue row or a badge. A passive site visit isn't the same kind of event as a scheduled call or an inbound lead, so it shouldn't compete for the same slot in the Opportunity Queue. Reps triage it on their own time; "Flag as opportunity action" is what promotes it into the queue.
    Keeps the queue reserved for things that already need a response today.
  2. 2
    Trigger = any matched site visit, not page-level tracking. That means this feed can tell you that a known buyer is back, but not what they looked at. "Known interest" below is pulled from their prior qualification history, not this visit. Upgrading to vendor/category page-view tracking would let us show real-time intent instead of inferred interest — worth scoping as a Phase 2 if the signal proves useful.
  3. 3
    Routes to the rep who last worked them. Preserves relationship continuity. Open question for Thursday: what happens when that rep is off territory, inactive, or it's been over 12 months — falls back to normal territory routing, but we need the exact threshold.
  4. 4
    Noise control. The same buyer won't resurface more than once per rolling 24 hours, even with multiple visits in that window, so one browsing session can't spawn duplicate cards.
  5. 5
    "Flag as opportunity action" pushes a new row into the Opportunity Queue — same shape as a call or lead reaching the queue today. This mock simulates the card moving to "Added to queue"; the real build should deep-link to the actual queue entry it creates.
  6. 6
    Concept only. No real identity-resolution or page-view pipeline is wired in here — every card and the "+ Simulate a new visit" button are illustrative. Flag / Snooze / Dismiss only change local state in this mock; nothing is written back anywhere.
Open items for Thursday: confirm the 24-hour dedupe window, the rep-routing fallback rule, and whether this earns its own left-nav spot (shown here under Buyer Accounts) or lives inside the Opportunity Queue as a tab. Reuses the same buyer score, chip, and panel styles as the Buyer Summary mock so both can ship together.
